Namebound Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The main copy of this policy is now at namebound.rhasan.online/privacy.

Namebound is a game for Android made by Daddy-Ousen ("we"). This page tells you what data the game uses.

Short version

Namebound has no account of its own, and it works offline. You can sign in with Google Play Games to put your scores on leaderboards; this is optional (see Google Play Games). We do not collect, receive or sell any personal data. The game can show ads, but only when you tap an ad button to get a reward. The ad service, Google AdMob, collects some data from your phone (see Ads).

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress in a file on your phone. This file holds things like gold, cleared stages, gear, settings and quest progress. The file stays on your phone. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the game, Android deletes the file.

Internet

The game downloads one small public file (a list of weekly challenges and monthly events) from this site. It saves a copy on your phone and works offline without it. The game sends no data about you with this request. Like any web request, the host (GitHub Pages) can see your IP address. We do not receive or store it. The game sends no other data to us. Ads and Google Play Games are the only other internet uses (see Ads and Google Play Games).

Ads

The game can show rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you tap an ad button (for example, to double your gold after a run). The game never forces an ad on you, and it shows no ads during a fight.

When the game loads or shows an ad, Google's ad code on your phone collects data and shares it with Google and its ad partners: your IP address (to guess your general area), your Android advertising ID and app set ID, how you use the ads (for example taps and video views), and app performance data (for example load time and crashes). Google uses this data to show ads, to measure them and to stop fraud. We do not receive this data. Google explains how it uses data from apps here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before it loads ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Ads in the game are limited to content rated for teens (T) or lower.

Google Play Games

The game uses Google Play Games Services for leaderboards. You do not need it to play.

If Play Games is set up on your phone, it can sign you in by itself when the game starts. You can also tap "Sign in to Play Games" on the Endless stage cards or the weekly challenge page.

When you are signed in, the game sends your Endless scores and weekly challenge scores to Google's leaderboards. Other players can see your score with your Play Games name and avatar, as your Play Games profile settings allow. Google also collects analytics and diagnostics data for the Play Games service.

Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive it; we only see the leaderboards, like other players. You can change who sees your profile, or delete your Play Games profile and its data, in the Play Games app or at myaccount.google.com.

Permissions

The game asks Android for these permissions. Vibrate: it shakes the phone when your hero gets hit or levels up (you can turn this off in Settings). Internet and network state: it downloads the events file described above and loads ads. Advertising ID: the ad service reads your advertising ID (see Ads).

Google Play

You get the game from Google Play. Google can collect data about the install and the download under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We only see the summary reports that Google gives to all app makers.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and up. It is not made for children, and we tell Google that it is not. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version adds something that uses data (for example purchases or cloud saves), we will update this page before that version comes out. The date at the top shows the last change.
